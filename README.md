# WHO Global Health Opportunity Analyzer 🏥🌐

> Python analyzer for high-value opportunities in the global health and WHO ecosystem

Maps the enormous opportunity landscape across global health — pandemic preparedness, digital health platforms, vaccine distribution networks, and healthcare data analytics — all tied to WHO initiatives and the $8T+ global healthcare market.

## What It Does
- Models ROI for global health technology businesses
- Analyzes pandemic preparedness and early warning system markets
- Maps digital health and telemedicine opportunity sizing
- Identifies vaccine logistics and cold chain infrastructure plays
- Surfaces mental health technology opportunities

```bash
git clone https://github.com/BlockchainNooberz/who-global-health-analyzer
```
